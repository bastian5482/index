ABOUT LYNXORA

Clarity. Control. Confidence.

LYNXORA is a market-information and internal account interface. Public cryptocurrency prices can be explored without registration. The current account functions are limited to internal EUR/USD balances.

This project does not currently provide cryptocurrency exchange execution, on-chain transfers, or custody of cryptoassets.

2. Data processed

The account flow sends the 12-word phrase to the LYNXORA mnemonic-auth Edge Function for authentication. Profile details (first/last name, date of birth, country and address), internal EUR/USD balances and transaction history are stored in Supabase. The phrase is used for account authentication and is sent to the mnemonic-auth function; it is not a blockchain private key or proof of cold-storage custody. The operator must verify logging and retention settings and must not store the phrase in profile tables. The operator must verify the complete authentication and logging configuration before production.

3. Service providers

Known technical services in this build include GitHub Pages for static hosting, Supabase for authentication/database/Edge Functions, Binance public market data for EUR spot prices, and Photon/OpenStreetMap-based geocoding for address suggestions. External providers process connection metadata under their own terms. Confirm regions, contracts and transfer safeguards.

4. Purposes and legal basis

The operator must document the purpose and legal basis for each category of data, especially identity and date-of-birth information, and collect only what is necessary.

5. Retention and rights

The operator must specify retention periods and provide a process for access, correction, deletion, restriction and complaints under applicable law.
